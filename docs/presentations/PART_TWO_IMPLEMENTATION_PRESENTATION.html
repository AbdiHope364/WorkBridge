SECTION 1: INTRODUCTION

Introduction & Context

Ethiopian Labor Landscape

Ethiopia has a rapidly expanding skilled trade workforce, yet over 65% of blue-collar transactions (electrical, plumbing, masonry, carpentry) operate informally.

The WorkBridge Vision

An enterprise-grade, multi-tenant digital marketplace connecting certified tradesmen directly with households, enterprises, and hiring organizations.

Strategic Engineering Goals

Provide transparent pricing, verified worker profiles, direct booking lifecycles, and a sustainable two-sided freemium business model.

SECTION 2: PROBLEM STATEMENT

Problem Statement

Fragmented Discovery

Tradesmen gather at physical roadside junctions, leading to unpredictable day-to-day work and wasted productive time.

Verification & Trust Deficit

Employers cannot verify past work quality, safety standards, identity records, or customer ratings before hiring.

Pricing Disparities & Transaction Gaps

Arbitrary pricing models with absence of standardized hourly wage benchmarks or digital booking histories.

SECTION 3: PROJECT OBJECTIVES

Project Objectives

General Objective

Design, implement, test, and deploy a secure, high-performance digital labor marketplace platform tailored for Ethiopia.

Monorepo & Portal Architecture

Architect a unified Turborepo workspace serving Jobseeker, Employer, and Admin portals with shared design systems.

Freemium Monetization & Security

Implement automated quota enforcement, simulated Ethiopian mobile payments (Telebirr, Chapa, CBE Birr), Google OAuth 2.0, and strict TypeScript type safety.

SECTION 4: SCOPE & LIMITATIONS

Scope and Limitations

System Scope (Functional)

Multi-role auth, trade worker search engine, interactive job board, direct bookings, conversational live chat, and admin moderation desk.

Simulated Payments Note

WorkBridge initially implements a simulated payment and subscription system for demonstration purposes before live production banking integration.

Geographical Focus & Web-First

Initial rollout optimized for urban hubs (Addis Ababa, Dire Dawa, Hawassa, Adama) with native mobile apps planned for Phase 2.

SECTION 5: SPECIFICATION

System Specification (FR & NFR)

Functional Requirements (FR)

Role-based auth, trade profile management, job posting wizard, candidate screening, direct booking workflows, and subscription checkout.

Performance & Scalability (NFR)

Sub-second page rendering using Next.js 16 SSR, MongoDB Atlas distributed indexing, and Turbo build caching.

Security & Maintainability (NFR)

Role-Based Access Control (RBAC), bcrypt hashing, HTTP-only JWTs, modular @repo/ui components, and shared @repo/types DTOs.

SECTION 7: METHODOLOGY

9-Phase Engineering Methodology

Phases 1–3: Foundation, Auth & Users

Monorepo configuration, Express API & MongoDB Atlas, JWT & Google OAuth 2.0, Forgot/Reset password, Worker & Client profiles.

Phases 4–6: Marketplace, Booking & Chat

Worker discovery & filters, job posting & apply, direct booking state transitions, reviews/ratings, and conversational live messaging.

Phases 7–9: Monetization, Admin & Quality

Freemium quotas (5 worker / 3 employer), simulated Telebirr/Chapa/CBE payments, Admin revenue monitoring, 100% strict TypeScript types & ESLint.
